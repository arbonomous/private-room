How is this private?

Video, voice and code go straight between your browsers, and people only get in if their link holds the room key (the part after the #, which is never sent to any server). Code is encrypted with that key. Video and voice use the browser's built-in WebRTC encryption. A free public introduction server helps browsers find each other and sees connection details such as IP addresses, but not your key or your content.

AI on your computer

Free and private. The AI runs on your own computer, so your code is never sent anywhere. The first time it downloads a big file (several GB), then it is saved. Keep this tab open while it loads. If the model does not fit your computer it switches to a smaller one by itself.

Cloud AI (optional, off by default)

Heads up: with this on, your questions and your code are sent to OpenRouter and the free model company, outside this private room. Free providers may keep or learn from what you send. Do not use it for anything private.

The key stays in this browser. It is never put in the shared code, never sent to the other people in the room, and only goes to openrouter.ai. The free plan allows about 50 requests a day, so automatic fixes try at most 2 times here.
